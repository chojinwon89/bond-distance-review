Read this as intrinsic binding strength, not just failure. A large median distance can be physically correct — saturated alkanes, ethers, and N₂ on noble metals genuinely physisorb 3.6–4 Å away (0% “detached” misfires). The real red flags are molecules that should chemisorb close but don’t — see the flagged list and the “detached on magnetic metals” column below.
