GOAD+SevenNet vs DFT — method validation

Quantitative evidence that GOAD + SevenNet-OMNI can stand in for high-throughput DFT: geometry, binding site, energy ranking, applicability domain and screening efficiency.
← gallery · binding energy · structure & geometry · method validation · single-point DFT vs ML · SevenNet vs MatterSim · likely failures
THESIS GOAD+SevenNet-OMNI reproduces the DFT adsorption geometry almost exactly — for chemisorbed adsorbates the binding site is identical in 100% of cases and the metal–adsorbate bond length agrees to 0.08 Å — and preserves the DFT binding-energy ordering well enough to use as a screening pre-filter. Absolute energies carry a ~0.5 eV scatter (comparable to the spread between DFT functionals), so the recommended workflow is MLIP for structure search + candidate ranking, DFT to confirm the short list.
