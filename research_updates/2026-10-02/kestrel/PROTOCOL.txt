Kestrel independent pymatgen DFT campaign, prepared 2 October 2026

Destination: /projects/ccpc/jcho/pymatgen/<surface>_<molecule>/PBE_D3/<start>/
Shared references: /projects/ccpc/jcho/pymatgen/_references/<name>/PBE_D3/
Control files: /projects/ccpc/jcho/pymatgen/_campaigns/20261002_pbe_d3/

Status at packaging: staged on Perlmutter; NOT submitted on Kestrel.
Kestrel external endpoint responded but rejected noninteractive authentication.

Scope: Ag111, Pt111, Pd111, Ir111, Cu111 crossed with glycerol, formate,
formic_acid, methane, O2, NH3, acetaldehyde, methanol, propanol (1-propanol),
and ethanol. All 50 systems have independent top, bridge and hollow starts.
There are 660 complex relaxations, 5 slab references, 10 gas references and
4 optional scaling tests. No GOAD/MLIP adsorption coordinates or energies are
used to select these starting structures. Existing isolated molecular inputs
and independent slab CIFs are reused and their hashes are recorded.

Slabs: four layers, lower two fixed; original archive cells retained.
Ag, Pt, Pd: 36 metal atoms; Cu, Ir: 64 metal atoms. This preserves each
surface's existing comparison cell. Different lateral cells imply different
coverage across metals; do not assume identical coverage for cross-metal trends.
Complexes range from 38 to 78 atoms. The same slab is used for all ten molecules
on a given metal, and its reference has the identical cell and fixed layers.

PBE+D3 zero damping (IVDW=11), as in the earlier pymatgen campaign:
ENCUT=450 eV; EDIFF=1e-6; EDIFFG=-0.05 eV/A; NELM=300; NSW=300;
IBRION=1; POTIM=0.2; ISIF=2; ALGO=Normal; ISPIN=2 for slabs/complexes;
ISMEAR=1; SIGMA=0.05; LREAL=A; VDW_S6=1; VDW_SR=1.217.
All slabs/complexes use the same Monkhorst-Pack 2x2x1 grid and zero shift.
Every complex and its references use byte-identical species POTCAR blocks.
New reference calculations avoid inheriting historical potential mismatches.
NCORE=2 for complexes/slabs. NBANDS is set per composition and is divisible
by 26 so 26- and 52-rank runs use the same number of bands.

Gas references: 20-A cubic box, Gamma-only, ISMEAR=0, SIGMA=0.01, LREAL=false,
no constraints. Closed-shell species use ISPIN=1. O2 uses ISPIN=2 and NUPDOWN=2
(triplet); neutral CHO2 uses ISPIN=2 and NUPDOWN=1 (doublet). Formate here names
an adsorbed CHO2 fragment in a neutral simulation cell; no extra electron or
periodic charged-cell background is added. The gas reference is therefore
neutral CHO2, not aqueous HCOO-minus. This differs from older spin-unpolarized
gas files; older O2/formate adsorption energies cannot be mixed without a
reference correction. Adsorbed systems are spin-polarized but have no fixed
total spin. These runs do not exhaust possible surface spin solutions.

Placement: every oxygen anchor, nitrogen for NH3, carbon for methane. O2's
equivalent ends are represented once. Tilts are 0/60 degrees, 0/60/90 for
formate, 0/90 for O2. Initial anchor height is 2.5 A above the local site plane,
raised only to keep every molecular atom >=1.8 A above that plane. This is a
vertical placement height, not an imposed final metal-adsorbate distance.
During relaxation the adsorbate is unconstrained. Initial internal geometry,
periodic-image separations and slab-contact collisions were checked.
All three glycerol oxygens are sampled. This first campaign uses one isolated
conformer; no exhaustive torsional or multi-anchor search is claimed.

Resource plan: one shared node per job, 26 MPI ranks, one CPU per rank,
2000 MB per CPU (~52 GB/job), OMP_NUM_THREADS=1. Gas references use 13 ranks.
Requested walltime is 12 h; individual jobs are submitted without arrays.
The submitter creates at most 20 dependency lanes, limiting simultaneous
production jobs to 20 (~five whole-node equivalents when all use 26 cores).
The shared partition permits unrelated jobs to share each physical node.
VASP module is pinned to vasp/6.4.2 and its executable hash is frozen after
the first successful Kestrel preflight. Verify module/account availability live.

Kestrel's published guidance supports quarter- to half-node VASP runs; 26 ranks
is a starting choice, not a measured optimum for this campaign. Four timing
inputs compare identical Ag111/glycerol and Ir111/glycerol geometries at 26
and 52 ranks with fixed NBANDS/NCORE and 20 SCF iterations. Timing-only jobs
are excluded from adsorption energies. Compare core-seconds as well as wall
time; use 52 cores only if its improvement warrants twice the resources.
Official documentation:
https://natlabrockies.github.io/HPC/Documentation/Applications/vasp/
https://natlabrockies.github.io/HPC/Documentation/Systems/Kestrel/Running/

After extracting the private bundle on Kestrel:
  cd /projects/ccpc/jcho/pymatgen/_campaigns/20261002_pbe_d3
  python3 kestrel_pymatgen_control.py audit
  python3 kestrel_pymatgen_control.py submit --scaling --execute --lanes 4
  # After the four timing jobs finish, inspect timings before production:
  python3 kestrel_pymatgen_control.py scaling-report
  python3 kestrel_pymatgen_control.py submit --execute --lanes 20
  python3 kestrel_pymatgen_control.py report

Without --execute the submitter only audits and prints a plan. Submitting
requires the exact Kestrel destination, shared partition, ccpc association,
and the pinned VASP executable. Every input is hashed and verified before
submission and again before execution. Existing output files and recorded
submissions are never overwritten. Ambiguous scheduler responses stop
submission for manual reconciliation, preventing accidental duplicate jobs.

The transfer archive contains licensed POTCAR files. Keep it within the
authorized user's private HPC storage; do not commit it to public GitHub.
Submit only from an authenticated Kestrel session. No Perlmutter jobs are
launched by these scripts. The archive does not replace existing Kestrel data.

Results: each job writes result.json and CONTCAR; collector writes results.csv
and lowest_converged.json. E_ads = E_complex - E_slab - E_gas, only when all
three same-campaign components are verified converged. Total energy is the
VASP free-energy TOTEN, consistent with the existing comparison convention.
Sigma-to-zero energy is retained separately. Lowest-converged geometry is
not proof of a global minimum; final bonding/dissociation needs structural
inspection before calling it an intact adsorption state. Scaling outputs
are excluded. Archive all variants, not just the lowest-energy outcome.
