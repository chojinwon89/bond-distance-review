Ag(111)-glycerol: matched GOAD initialization comparison on CPU

Allocation: Perlmutter CPU interactive, account m5281, one node, up to 4 hours.
Job: 59224069. Node at launch: nid200253.

20 searches: seeds 101,202,303,404,505,606,707,808,909,1010 for each of
(1) baseline: original GOAD initialization;
(2) contact25: translate the molecule vertically in each original initial
    candidate until the minimum distance over all O-Ag pairs is 2.5 Angstrom.

This is GOAD without charge guidance. Original initialization places the
lowest O 2.3 Angstrom vertically above the uppermost slab atom; lateral offset
means that vertical height is not the nearest O-Ag interatomic distance.
The new initialization preserves x/y positions, orientation, torsions and
internal geometry. All three molecular oxygen atoms (indices 0,3,5, zero-based)
are eligible to be the closest contact. These are not three separately forced
anchor campaigns. Counts for every seed are in initialization_checks.json.
No O-Ag constraint or projection is applied after population initialization.
The original GOAD mutation bounds remain unchanged in both groups.

Model: SevenNet-MF, 7net-mf-ompa / omat24, device=cpu, four Torch threads.
Model parameter hash is checked against the historical GPU campaign.
Slab/molecule geometries are copied from the same independent relaxed
references used before. Their energies are recalculated on CPU for both arms.
The old seed202 endpoint is also checked against its saved GPU energy before
the search begins. This is not a new DFT calculation.

GOAD settings: population 60, generations upper limit 200, mutation .3,
crossover .7, elite 5, one worker per search, hard cap 1500 model evaluations.
GOAD may also stop under its existing generation/stagnation criteria.
The best evaluated search candidate receives final BFGS relaxation with a
200-evaluation cap and force threshold 0.05 eV/Angstrom. Bottom two slab layers
remain fixed. No distance constraint is applied to the molecule.

Initialization tests check: unmodified baseline geometries; identical random
number states after setup; contact25 differs only by rigid vertical translation;
all 60 nearest O-Ag distances are 2.5 Angstrom; all three O anchors occur per seed.
Each worker saves all initial structures, contact indices/distances, search
trace, pre-relaxation best geometry, final geometry, final forces/convergence,
connectivity/admissibility audit, model/source provenance and completion marker.

Twenty srun tasks share the allocation; this is not a Slurm array. Each task
has 12 logical CPUs (6 physical cores), with four computational threads.
The launcher generates all_results.csv, paired_comparison.csv, comparison.png,
comparison.pdf, best_endpoints.json, status.json and worker_exit_code.txt once
the workers finish, then the interactive shell releases the allocation.

Interpretation: compare only valid relaxed endpoints, report all failures,
and retain seed-level differences. More favorable MLIP energy alone does not
establish a better DFT structure. Distinct promising endpoints can subsequently
be validated using the same DFT settings and matched component references.
