# Independent BEEF-vdW adsorption comparison

Submitted 71 individual CPU regular jobs on October 2, 2026: 60 original pymatgen starts (Ag111 acetaldehyde 8, Ag111 DME 8, Cu111 DME 10, Cu111 ethanol 10, Ag111 glycerol 24), five saved GOAD endpoints, and six deduplicated references (Ag111/Cu111 slabs; acetaldehyde/DME/ethanol/glycerol gas molecules). Job IDs and paths are in job_index.csv. These are the same five systems as the independent PBE+D3 campaign; this submission does not expand to Pd/Rh/Ir.

All adsorbed-system POSCAR, POTCAR and KPOINTS files are byte-identical to the corresponding original PBE+D3 inputs. The starting structures are the original pymatgen placements or saved GOAD endpoints, not the PBE+D3-relaxed structures. BEEF-vdW is specified as GGA=BF, LUSE_VDW=.TRUE., ZAB_VDW=-1.8867, LASPH=.TRUE.; all D3 settings are removed. ENCUT=450 eV, EDIFF=1e-6, EDIFFG=-0.05 eV/A, IBRION=1, POTIM=0.2, ISIF=2; bottom-layer constraints are retained. Gas references use 20-A cubic boxes, Gamma sampling, ISPIN=1, ISMEAR=0, SIGMA=0.01, and LREAL=.FALSE. Slab and complex cells, slab meshes, compositions and PAW block contents are verified to match.

The archived BEEF files inspected during preparation report Zab_VDW=-0.8491. Those outputs remain untouched. This campaign uses the documented BEEF-vdW value -1.8867 and fresh references; its component energies must not be combined with the older archive. This difference alone does not establish the cause of any previously observed adsorption-energy discrepancy. Glycerol's inspected archived gas reference also lacked ionic convergence.

Each job requests one regular CPU node, 128 MPI ranks and two hours. Five minutes before walltime, a signal requests VASP's LSTOP after an ionic step. After a clean stop, a valid CONTCAR with matching composition, cell and fixed atoms is carried into a new immutable attempt directory; the job requeues itself. A maximum of three two-hour segments (six hours) is allowed, or six segments (twelve hours) for glycerol. Wavefunctions are not retained: electronic calculations restart from atomic densities. Corrupt checkpoints, failed electronic convergence, abnormal exits and stale locks stop for inspection instead of blindly retrying. Checkpoint/requeue, final convergence and duplicate protection passed an integration test with saved outputs and a simulated scheduler; real runtime status is recorded separately in each job directory.

No existing queued job was canceled or migrated. Both regular and preempt two-hour dry-run submissions received roughly October 10 start estimates at preparation time; estimates are not reservations and may change. Most older jobs were waiting on Priority; the CPU partition snapshot showed 2955 allocated, 3 idle and 44 other nodes. The current m5281 association does not include premium. Kestrel SSH name resolution was unavailable from this session.

For results, inspect completion_result.json and its output_directory for each job. Only electronically and ionically converged outputs with a normal VASP exit are completed. Compute adsorption energies only after both new reference jobs converge, using E_complex - E_slab - E_molecule. Select the lowest converged structure separately within each functional; do not rank total energies across functionals. All original inputs, hashes, attempts and submission records are preserved.

Sources: [VASP BEEF-vdW settings](https://vasp.at/wiki/Nonlocal_vdW-DF_functionals), [NERSC scheduling](https://docs.nersc.gov/jobs/scheduling/), [NERSC queues](https://docs.nersc.gov/jobs/policy/), [NERSC VASP builds](https://docs.nersc.gov/applications/vasp/).
