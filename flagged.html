What this list is. A geometric screen, not a verdict: each row is a structure whose adsorbate is too far from the metal to be bound, for a molecule that established surface chemistry says binds that metal. High-confidence = small molecules with unambiguous chemistry (CO, NO, CO₂, O₂, H₂O, NH₂, H₂S, SO₂, radicals). Medium = O-containing organics on oxophilic Cr/Fe/Co/Ni. Re-relax these (ideally with an OC20-trained/fine-tuned checkpoint) before trusting them.
Flagging rules
  • Binds all TMs (flag detached or borderline, any metal): CO, NO, NO₂, NH₂, H₂S, SO₂, DMSO, thiophene, HCO, formate, carbonate.
  • Strong on reactive metals (flag on Cr/Fe/Co/Ni): CO₂, N₂, O₂, H₂O.
  • O-organics (alcohols, acids, aldehydes, ketones, ethers, furans, phenols, sugars): flag when detached on oxophilic Cr/Fe/Co/Ni (med) or Cu (low).
  • Excluded (physisorb, detachment is physical): saturated alkanes and bare hydrocarbons; CO₂/N₂ on coinage/noble metals.
  • Distance = nearest metal–heavy-atom contact (min-image PBC). detached >3.6Å, borderline 3.0–3.6Å.
